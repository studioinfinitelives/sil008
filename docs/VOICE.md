# Voice

Read by `/release-notes` when it writes the changelog at infinitelives.io/changelog.

## Audience

Players and curious visitors of Studio Infinite Lives' games (Habi Sloth, Team EvL, Fantasy Apples). Not developers, not other studios.

## Tone

Warm, plain and brief. Say what a visitor can now see or do, and where. No hype, no jargon, no exclamation marks.

## Spelling

American English. Product names as written on the site: Habi Sloth, Team EvL, Fantasy Apples.

## Allowed outside names

Only the studio's own products and Studio Infinite Lives. No vendor, tool or framework names (hosting, analytics, build tools).

## Leave out

Refactors, tests, tooling, deploys, CDN and infrastructure changes. Privacy or cookie changes are included only when they change what a visitor agrees to.
